# Klubbnettside

Interactive prototype of a website platform for Norwegian sports clubs, shown with the fictional multi-sport club **Oslo Sportsklubb** (fotball, sykkel, langrenn).

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

Opens on http://localhost:3100.

## Demo in five minutes

0. **Klubbytte** – brukermenyen i admin bytter mellom Oslo Sportsklubb (fleridrett) og Bærum og Omegn Cykleklubb (én idrett, fem grener). Menyen, forsiden, fargene og «Om klubben» endrer seg med klubben. BOC har egne farger (gul flate, blå tekst), Grasrotandelen-panel, innmelding i Spond og nyhetsarkiv hentet fra klubbens egen side.
1. **Forsiden** `/` – «Finn gruppen din» i hero (alder, gren, nivå), «Klubbåret» for BOC med ritt, Mallorca-turer og Zwift-vinter, «Finn din aktivitet», historier og logostripe. Enkeltøkter og påmelding ligger i Spond, så forsiden har ingen ukeoversikt.
2. **Laget** `/fotball/jenter/j16/j16-2` – en side generert fra hierarkiet, med «Sesongen 2026»: ukerytme, pauser, cuper og hvilket årskull som overtar ved nyttår.
3. **Barn og ungdom** `/barn-og-ungdom` – egen landingsside for foreldre: gruppene for barn gruppert per gren, med alder og treningstider. Voksengrupper som også tar imot ungdom står for seg selv.
4. **Treningstider og terminliste** `/aktiviteter?gruppe=j16-2` – ukerytmen for hver gruppe og datoene klubben har kunngjort, filtrert med de samme fargede grenetikettene som «Klubbåret» (alle vises, et trykk skjuler, «Vis alle» nullstiller) og alderen fra «Finn din aktivitet» (Alle aldre / Barn og ungdom / Voksne). Står én gren igjen, kan man velge avdeling › årsklasse › lag under den; det er der lenkene fra gruppesidene lander. Enkeltøkter og påmelding ligger i Spond; se `src/lib/timetable.ts` for hvorfor skillet går der.
5. **Publisering** `/admin/publiser` – bytt til Marte Solberg (lagleder J16-2) i brukermenyen, gjerne i mobilbredde.
6. **Personvern** `/admin/personer/p-nora` → *Anonymiser permanent*, og åpne så `/nyheter/j16-2-snudde-kampen-mot-lyn` på nytt.

Demo data can be reset from the user menu in admin (or by restarting the server).

## Architecture notes

- **Data**: typed mock store in memory (`src/lib/data/store.ts`), one per club, seeded from `src/lib/data/seed/`. Dates are relative to today, so «denne uken» always has content.
- **Schedule**: the site publishes the weekly rhythm (`TrainingSeries`) and the terminliste (dated matches, races, camps and open events). Single sessions, sign-ups and last-minute changes stay in the club's own planning tool — `src/lib/timetable.ts` holds the rule.
- **Times the club cannot promise**: not every session has a clock. A series that cannot promise one sets `startApprox`, keeps the earliest likely time as its sort key and says the rest in `note`; views with room print «ca.» before the range. The site never invents a start it cannot stand behind — `src/lib/timetable.ts`. BOC 1–4 no longer need it: they meet Tuesday and Thursday at 17.30 at Bekkestua torg and Saturday at 10.00 at Kaffebrenneriet, every week April–September except July, so each session is two series (April–June, August–September) and July stays empty everywhere.
- **Writes**: only through server actions in `src/app/actions.ts`, each checking permissions from `src/lib/permissions.ts`. Supabase would replace the store and `src/lib/session.ts`.
- **Hierarchy**: `src/lib/org.ts`. Levels below sport are optional; page templates are chosen by position in the tree. A level with a single group under it is skipped (`org.soleGroup`): its links go straight to the group, its own address forwards there, and the group's breadcrumb leaves it out — BOC's Banesykling holds only Banegruppa.
- **Barn og ungdom** (`/barn-og-ungdom`): the club from a parent's side, with its own title and description. A parent searching «sykkelklubb barn bærum» is navigating, not deciding, while the front page has to give an undecided adult a reason — one hero cannot do both without saying nothing. The search query never reaches the site, so the routing is done by giving the engine a better page to send them to, not by switching anything at runtime. `youthExplorer` in `src/lib/finder.ts` splits groups *for* children (their age range ends inside youth) from adult groups that also take someone young (open-ended, but entered at 16 or under) — «fra 17 år» really means 17 to 99, so folding those in would list the whole adult club as a children's offer. Clubs with no children's groups get no page and no link to one — `hasYouth` in `src/app/(public)/layout.tsx` decides, and the header, the phone sheet and the footer all follow it.
- **Group presenter**: every group page opens with the person who presents it, in a card of their own under its description (`GroupLead`: a large portrait, name, role, phone or e-mail and a link to all contacts) — the team manager (lagleder) if it has one, then its head coach, a coach, or the section lead it inherits (`presenterFor` in `src/lib/content.ts`) — with a link to the contacts. The card names the role in the club's own word, `leadTitle`, inherited down the tree: BOC says «Gruppeleder», and «Road Captain» on Landevei. A person's `portraitPhotoId` is shown there and in the contact list only while they are visible and have consented to photos (`portraitOf`); until then their initials stand in. No demo person has a portrait: several BOC names are the club's real volunteers, and a stock face must not stand in for a real person.
- **Dark pages**: a group with `pageTone: "dark"` renders its whole page dark (`.page-dark` in globals.css) — Zwift, ridden in the evening and often in a dark room. Like `.header-dark` it redefines the semantic tokens for the subtree, so no component needs to know; the ground is the club's dark header colour and links take the header's link colour.
- **Announcements**: a group can carry an `announcement` — a band in the club colour straight under its hero, for something its riders must not miss (Banegruppa's next course, Zwift's Fryd Vinterligaen). With `opensAt` it switches from `title` to `titleOpen` on the moment a sign-up opens, and it goes after `until`. With `inline` it is a compact note in the weekly plan instead (Zwift's Fryd Vinterligaen). A group can also leave out sections that say nothing for it (`hideSections`): Zwift drops the terminliste and the season summary.
- **Når og hvor**: a group with one simple rhythm (`simpleSchedule`, BOC 1–4) gets one section, above its terminliste, in place of the season summary and the weekly plan (`meet-up.tsx`): a card per meeting point with its days and clock set large and a way to get there, and the twelve months as a strip — season months filled, a break inside the season hatched and named. Its terminliste also carries the rides the group trains towards (`Race.groupIds`): at the next edition, projected from the organiser's last one until it is announced, with «ca.» in place of a weekday. A node can also name groups whose season belongs in its terminliste and every page below it (`seasonsInTerminliste`, `terminlisteSeasons` in `club-year.ts`): Landevei carries the Zwift season, linked to the Zwift group, since that is where its riders go for the winter.
- **Members**: an adult group lists its members one tile each (`MemberGrid`, section «Gruppa»): only people who are visible and 18 or older — children never, nor anyone whose age the club does not know — and only when there are at least four, since fewer reads as a registry gap. A tile shows the portrait with photo consent, otherwise initials. BOC 1 and 2 carry invented adult riders for it. The group's fact row shows a squad size only for football; a group can name its `seasonFocus` instead (BOC 1: Vätternrunden).
- **Privacy**: `src/lib/privacy.ts`. People are linked to content structurally (photo regions, text mentions with neutral wording, activity slots). Anonymisation rewrites the stored content permanently.
- **Design system**: tokens in `src/app/globals.css`, club colours as CSS variables per club. A club has two brand colours: `primary` paints surfaces and buttons (BOC's yellow), `link` is the same brand as text and must stay readable on white. Reference at `/design-system`.
- **Hero**: one band — the photograph, with the statement on it. That only works because two things left it: the finder, which now has a section of its own, and a headline that used to run to five lines. Together they forced a treatment that swallowed the picture, and the picture is what says what kind of club this is. The statement now ends at 56 % of the width while BOC's riders in club kit sit from 62 % out, so `.hero-scrim` (globals.css) treats the bottom-left corner and leaves them alone: a bottom-up tone and a second one fading out sideways. Colour only — blur was tried and dropped, because it costs photograph everywhere it reaches, so contrast against a light picture now rests on the gradients alone. A hero photo for this design needs its subject in the right half or high in the frame. Nothing is asked of anyone above the fold. Set `heroVideoUrl` on the club (a file in `public/`, or any URL) and the band plays film instead, with the hero photo as poster and fallback.
- **What the statement says** (`identity.headline` / `intro` per club): two jobs in one breath. The weekly training leads, because it is what a member actually gets — 28 of BOC's sessions a week are fellestrening, and «hele året» is the road through the summer and spinning and Zwift through the winter. The races and Mallorca follow, because an adult comparing the club to the free social rides nearby is not asking which group fits their age (the rides have levels too) but why join a club at all, and those are the part a ride cannot arrange. The supporting line names both audiences, so a parent knows in one sentence that they are in the right place. One low-commitment action follows it, «Se klubbåret» rather than «Bli medlem», with a second door to `/barn-og-ungdom`. Clubs without `identity.year` fall back to «Finn din aktivitet».
- **Front-page grounds**: the sections under the hero alternate white and light grey, counted over the sections a club actually shows (the club year, the editorial story and the kit are optional), so no two neighbours share a ground.
- **Klubbåret is written as an argument**, not a description: it has to say what a membership buys that turning up to a free group ride does not.
- **Group finder**: the card asks age, one or more branches (or sports) and level, with back and forward arrows; it skips any question whose answer would not change the result, and ranks groups by level distance, the club's pick (`recommendFirst`, set on Zwift) and age span (`src/components/public/group-finder.tsx`). Recommendations are dealt one per chosen branch before any second group, since a member can ride with a group in each. The result is budgeted to the card's fixed height, so nothing in the finder scrolls. It sits in its own section under the statement and the club's numbers, not in the hero: asking «how old are you?» before the page has given a reason gets an answer from nobody, and ODP puts its own levels below the fold for the same reason. Age is picked from bands (`AGE_BANDS`), not typed: a number field answers one person's question and hides the club's shape, while the ladder shows at a glance that the road groups take everyone from 17 upwards — 19–39, 40–59 and 60+ all return the same eleven groups. A band matches a group when the two ranges overlap, and every result row carries the group's own age label, so a partial overlap reads honestly. Bands with nothing in them are shown disabled, because «no group for a seven-year-old» is an answer too. The bands stand in two columns, Voksne first and Barn beside it (17–18 included), so a parent finds the child's column without reading the adult ladder. Groups declare the levels they welcome in `levels`; the scale in `src/lib/levels.ts` has three steps — Nybegynner, Har syklet en del, Aktiv mosjonist — and all of them sit below racing. Someone who already races knows which group is theirs; everyone else is placing themselves against people they have never met, and a fourth, faster step made the whole ladder read as a club for fast riders. The road groups list one or two adjacent steps, which is what lets the finder put BOC 4 first for a beginner and BOC 2 first for an active rider. Branch icons are drawn to Feather's grid in `branch-icons.tsx`. The one-line schedule on a group card (`scheduleSummary` in `src/lib/finder.ts`) only names a time or a place when every session of the group shares it.
- **Hero band**: edge to edge up to 1728 px — the width a 16" MacBook Pro reports — and always square and flush under the header. Above that the photograph keeps its 1728 px and the band carries on to the screen edges in the header's colour (`--header-bg`, black for BOC; the inverse surface for a club with a paper header), so the picture never stretches across an ultrawide display. It fills the viewport below the header (`100svh`), clamped between 38rem and 52rem, and settles at a fixed 38rem once the photograph stops at 1728 px. Below lg (1023 px and down) the band comes apart: the photograph alone, 4:3 across the screen width, then the statement and its actions on the header's colour, then the numbers two by two — so nothing sits on the picture and it needs no scrim. In that 2×2 the slants cut only the gap between the columns, the same in both rows, with a rule between the rows; the framing slants at the outer edges belong to the single row from lg. That is a letterbox on a wide screen, so the hero photo's `focal` decides which band of the picture survives the crop, and a club can set a second, taller photo for narrow screens (BOC uses `b-ph-hero-narrow` below 1025 px). Bundled photos are served at their own size (see `photo.tsx`), so a hero meant to go full bleed on a 16" retina screen wants a source around 3400 px wide; BOC's is 1536 and is upscaled there.
- **Guides**: the public pages carry no vertical column lines; every section's guide layer carries only the fact strip's slants on down the page (`.guides::before`): hairlines at the same −21.25° lean and the same spacing — a quarter of the content width from lg, a half below it — phased so each passes through a slash in the strip, and scrolling with the page. CSS draws them and sets the horizontal phase; `SlantGuides` (`components/ui/slant-guides.tsx`) measures where each section sits relative to the strip's last row and writes the vertical phase as `--slant-y`, again whenever the page changes height. The footer carries the same pattern as bands a shade darker than its own colour instead of lines (`.guides--bands`).
- **Partners**: the logo strip (`sponsors.tsx`) stands straight under the hero's numbers on the front page — after the club says who it is, who stands behind it — and again in the footer of every page, in `tone="inverse"` on the club's dark colour. BOC's partners use their own logo files (`src/components/assets/`, mapped by name in `sponsor-logos.tsx`); being white on transparent, each is a mask filled with the strip's colour, and each is sized to the same area rather than the same height, so a long wordmark does not outweigh a compact one. The other demo partners are invented and drawn.
- **Menu**: while the sports panel is open, everything under the header goes to frosted glass (a blurred, lightly tinted veil in `site-header.tsx`); a press on it closes the panel. A dark header (`.header-dark`) darkens every chip surface on the panel, the status tones included, so «Krever kurs» sits beside «Alle nivåer» rather than as the one pale chip.
- **No «tar inn nye»**: the site never says whether a group takes new members — not on a page, a card, a filter or in the finder's ranking, and there is no field for it. A club short of members is not served by groups that look closed; every group reads as one you can try.
- **Carousels**: when a row of group cards holds more than fits, it shows three and a half on desktop and two and a bit on tablets, as Stripe does, so the card cut off at the edge says there is more; the paging buttons alone were easy to miss. A row that fits keeps whole cards.
- **Articles**: a body is paragraphs, quotes and photos, plus subheadings, lists and links in running text for a notice with several parts (BOC's member benefits at Anton Sport, `/nyheter/medlemsfordeler-hos-anton-sport`). Lists are inline text like paragraphs, so anonymisation reaches a name in a list too. A club can add links under «Klubben» in the footer (`footerLinks`) and a fixed front-page section on its kit (`kit`: BOC's Kalas drops, announced in Spond, linking to the member benefits).
- **Radius**: tight — 2 / 3 / 5 / 8 / 12 px for xs–xl (`--radius-*` in globals.css; admin 4 / 6 / 8 for md–xl), buttons square. Circles and pills (`rounded-full`) are not on the scale.
- **Buttons**: flat, square (`--radius-button: 0`), no drop shadow. A theme can give buttons their own colour with `action` (BOC: teal with white text, while yellow stays the surface colour).
- **Branch colours**: each branch (the sport, in a multi-sport club) has a colour pair from the `--cat-*` tokens, each pair ≥ 5:1, fixed by its place in the club's order (`categoryStyle` in `category-filter.tsx`). «Finn din aktivitet» and Treningstider og terminliste filter with the same `CategoryFilter` as «Klubbåret». Both share the age choice in `age-choice.tsx` — Alle aldre / Barn og ungdom / Voksne, sorted by whom a group is *for*: BOC 1–4 take riders from 17 but are adult groups, so they sit under Voksne only. The front page has no week view: single sessions and sign-up live in Spond. «Klubbåret» filters by the same labels, one per branch in the same order and colours: Spinning and Zwift are both Innendørs, and the training trips go with their groups' branch (BOC's are Landevei) and are drawn in that branch's row, among its races. Every period — a season or a trip — links to `/aktiviteter` filtered to its group (`#treningstider` for a season, `#terminliste` for a trip), carries a border in the branch colour and glows on hover. In both, every branch starts shown, a press takes it away and «Vis alle» brings them back — in «Finn din aktivitet» its groups leave the list, in «Klubbåret» its rows leave the chart rather than dimming. A filtered-away label loses its colour and keeps only an outline. In the chart a season band is its branch's label drawn out over the months — the same background and text colour — while race dots and trip marks, too small to carry a pale fill, take the label's text colour; the key under the filter shows only shape (published, projected, the club's own race).
- **Klubbåret**: races (`db.races`), camps and `seasonal` training series on one twelve-month axis (`src/lib/club-year.ts`). Race dates are the organiser's latest published edition; past editions are projected a year ahead and shown as unconfirmed, never as the organiser's date. Seasons that touch are merged, so a programme that runs all year (BOC's banesykling) is one band, «hele året», rather than two split at New Year. The section appears when the club has written `identity.year`.
- **News wording**: the «Nyheter» subtitle names what the club's stories are, from `identity.newsKinds`. The default says «Kampreferater»; BOC says «Referater», because a cycling club has no matches.
- **Join wizard**: a group whose joining means setting up an app can give its «Praktisk» section a `participation.wizard` — the steps one at a time with screenshots, a progress bar, back and next, and a last step pointing on (`wizardDone`). Zwift's follows its presentation: equipment, follow the group lead in Zwift Companion, accept the invitation, join when the session starts, choose the day's workout. The step you are on is remembered in the browser, because the wizard sends you off to the app and you come back (`join-wizard.tsx`).
- **Presentations**: `/presentasjon/zwift-2025-26` is a 16:9 deck in the browser (`components/deck/deck.tsx`) — the Zwift group's plan, how to sign up in Zwift Companion, and the season 2025/26 in numbers — outside the public layout, in the club's colours and type. Slides sit on a fixed 1600 × 900 stage scaled to the window; arrows, space, Home/End, a click on either third or a swipe move between them, F is full screen, and the slide number is in the address. Its numbers are computed from `lib/data/zwift-2025-26.ts`, where the sources are written down: riders per session from Zwift Companion's Meetups, different riders from Spond as a minimum — counts only, no names. The Zwift page links to it.
- **Photo crop**: a photo has a `focal` point and an optional `zoom` (scale on top of cover-fit, in `.photo-canvas`). A photo that needs a different crop in one context gets its own record over the same file — BOC's road covers (`b-ph-landevei-hero`) are the hero's narrow cut, closer and lowered so the riders sit above the «Neste aktivitet» card.
- **Photos**: development images from Unsplash; the regions marking people are placed by hand.
- **Kit as garments**: `kit.jerseys` (`jersey-showcase.tsx`) shows the club kit as transparent product cut-outs on a diagonal wash of the club's own colours — `--club-secondary` to the header's dark, with a soft glow of `--club-primary` behind and the site's own hairline lean at low opacity — rather than a photograph. Falls back to `kit.photoId`'s plain `Photo` for a club without cut-outs. BOC's source renders had a flat studio background, cut to alpha with a colour-distance threshold against the corner colour (script not checked in; rerun it if a new garment render arrives).
